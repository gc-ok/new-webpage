Custom applications can streamline approval flows, eligibility processes, testing operations, intervention tracking, and internal staff tools.

The Challenge

Schools often have high-value workflows that no vendor really serves well.

  • The workflow is too school-specific
  • The tool requires too many workarounds
  • Staff still rely on spreadsheets and manual approvals
  • Important systems do not talk to one another
What a better solution looks like

Built around the actual user and process.

  • Clear interfaces staff can learn quickly
  • Automated steps where it matters most
  • Reporting and tracking built into the workflow
  • Less friction and fewer manual handoffs

Examples of the kinds of systems we can build for schools.

At-Risk / MTSS hubs

Track grades, behavior, attendance, and intervention history over time in one clearer internal system.

Eligibility trackers

Auto-send daily updates to coaches and attendance staff so eligibility stays visible without manual follow-up.

Discipline & ISI dashboards

Move away from paper-based behavior tracking and log incidents, trends, and follow-up digitally.

Walkthrough automations

Generate and email teacher feedback instantly from walkthrough forms instead of cleaning up notes manually.

Graduation, testing, payroll, and signature workflows

Build systems for graduation and credit tracking, testing center scheduling and packets, sub and payroll tools, or digital agreement collection and logging.
