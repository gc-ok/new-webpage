The Gap

Many schools have the data, but not a clear intervention story.

State testing data, local assessments, attendance, grades, behavior, subgroup trends, and intervention logs are often available. The hard part is turning that mix into usable answers about risk, response, and next steps.

What you actually need

Decision-ready interpretation.

That means cleaner reporting, stronger context, and findings that help schools decide who needs support, where gaps are showing up, and which interventions seem to be making a difference.

Analysis Types

Analysis built around the questions school leaders actually ask.

State testing and longitudinal analysis

Track how students, campuses, grade levels, or programs perform over time instead of relying on a single testing cycle.

Cohort analysis

Follow a graduating class, intervention group, or program cohort across years to see where progress holds and where it slips.

Predictive and regression analysis

Explore which variables appear most tied to course failure, chronic absenteeism, behavior, assessment performance, or graduation risk.

Demographic and subgroup analysis

Understand how performance differs across student groups, campuses, grades, classrooms, programs, or accountability categories.

MTSS and at-risk analysis

Combine grades, attendance, behavior, assessment, and intervention history to identify students who may need Tier 2 or Tier 3 support.

Assessment and intervention reporting

Translate complex data into cleaner visuals, clearer summaries, and decision-ready reporting for principals, counselors, interventionists, and district leaders.
