Privacy Policy

GC Education Analytics LLC values your privacy. This policy explains how we handle data across two areas of our business: website visitors and prospective clients, and client-hosted software solutions.

1. Information We Collect

When you contact us, request information, or book a consultation, we may collect your name, school or district name, email address, job title, and any additional information you voluntarily include in your message.

2. How We Use This Information

We use this information only for legitimate business purposes, including responding to your inquiry, scheduling consultations, following up about requested services, and sending relevant updates or resources when appropriate.

3. Third-Party Tools Used on the Website

We currently use trusted third-party services to help operate our public website and communication flow, including Cloudflare for site delivery, Calendly for scheduling, and FormSubmit for contact form handling.

Please avoid including confidential student information in public web forms.

4. Client-Hosted Software and Student Data

Many GC Education Analytics solutions are designed around a privacy-first, client-hosted model.

  • Software may be deployed and executed within the school or district's own Google Workspace environment.
  • Student and staff data can remain inside the client-controlled environment rather than being transferred to GC Education Analytics-owned infrastructure.
  • We design solutions to minimize unnecessary data movement and preserve school control where possible.

5. FERPA and Student Data

For solutions that run within the client's environment, GC Education Analytics does not need to maintain copies of student personally identifiable information on vendor-owned servers. The school or district retains control over access, storage, and retention of its own operational and student data.

6. Security

Security is a shared responsibility. We aim to use sound development practices and request only the access needed for the work, while clients remain responsible for permissions, data access, and platform settings within their environment.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

For website inquiries, we retain contact information only as long as reasonably necessary for business follow-up and recordkeeping. For client-hosted software, retention of operational and student data is controlled by the client environment and policies, not by GC Education Analytics.

8. Contact

If you have privacy questions or want to request deletion of your business contact information from our records, email gcastillo@gceducationanalytics.com.
