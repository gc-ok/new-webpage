How this started

It began when Gerardo's wife, a choir teacher, was getting ready for the school year and asked for something that would make day-to-day student tracking easier. Student Tracker Pro came out of that conversation.

Open the Student Tracker

Track daily participation, objectives, and comments directly from your phone, tablet, or laptop.

Student Tracker Pro combines a Google Sheets backend with a simple web interface so teachers can manage classroom data quickly and cleanly.

Fast data entry and a teacher-friendly dashboard.

Class-level organization and comment tracking.

Theme customization and setup options.

Simple, responsive views for on-the-go use.

Key Features
  • Real-time participation and objective tracking
  • Google Sheets backend inside your own account
  • Automated sheet creation by class
  • Predefined and custom comments
  • Personalized web app tied to your sheet
  • Over 20 theme options
How It Works
  • Make a copy of the provided Google Sheet
  • Run the initial setup from the custom menu
  • Enter your name and number of classes
  • Let the script create your tracking and comments sheets
  • Deploy the Apps Script as a personal web app
  • Use the tracker from your own school device
Important FERPA note: you are responsible for restricting access to your Google Sheet after entering student information. GC Education Analytics does not have access to your student data. The tool operates within your own Google account.
