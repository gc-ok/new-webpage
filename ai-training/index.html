What It Is

AI training centered on building real school tools.

Participants learn how to describe the workflow, logic, and outcome they need so AI can help them build more useful school tools.

Why Apps Script

Google Workspace is already the operational backbone for many schools.

That makes Apps Script a practical place to build automations and lightweight internal apps without introducing a giant new platform.

Privacy-first angle: the training is centered on tools that can run inside your Google Workspace environment, helping keep school data under your control.
