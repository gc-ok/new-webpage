Mission

Help schools streamline data processes, automate routine work, and create more time for the human side of leadership and teaching.

What that means

Better workflows, clearer reporting, earlier intervention insight.

The goal is not to make schools adapt to generic software. It is to build tools and reporting that fit how schools actually operate.

Founder Story

Why this work matters to Gerardo Castillo.

Gerardo Castillo came into this work through education first. After graduating from Harvard in 2018 and serving with Teach For America in Oklahoma City, he continued working as a classroom teacher.

That experience made one thing obvious: schools are full of high-capacity people doing important work inside systems that are often more manual, fragmented, and time-consuming than they should be.

GC Education Analytics was built at the intersection of classroom understanding, analytical training, and a strong belief that better systems can free educators to focus more on students and less on process overhead.

"How can data and technology work for schools without creating more burden for the people already carrying the work?"

That question sits underneath every project, whether the job is a dashboard, automation, staff tool, or training engagement.

A mix of dashboards, internal workflows, and leadership-facing tools shaped around the daily realities of school operations.

School Context

The work stays close to the realities of daily school operations.

That means thinking about the actual staff using the tool, the routines they already have, the pressure points in the workflow, and the way data needs to be interpreted on the ground.

The best systems usually sit at the intersection of student support, staff communication, intervention follow-up, and leadership visibility.
